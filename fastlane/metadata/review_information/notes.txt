Workbench's iPhone app is a companion to Workbench, a free Mac app. It connects to Workbench on the user's own Mac over their private Tailscale network, so it can't connect to anything without that setup.

To review the app without a Mac, tap "Try a Demo" on the first screen. The demo shows sample projects served from inside the app: a session that updates every few seconds, one waiting for Allow or Deny, a finished agent with a pull request, and changed files with diffs. You can reply to a session, tap Allow or Deny, and browse changes and files. Exit the demo from Settings (the gear button at the top of the project list).

The camera is used only to scan the pairing code shown in Workbench on the Mac. The app collects no data.
